More Than a Board. It's a Moment.

Hi, we're Mily & Dan!

What you might call professional food lovers, amateur everything else. Give us a delicious meal, a great glass of wine, or a weekend cocktail, and we're in our element.

But our love for food came with a little plot twist: we have quite a few dietary restrictions. So, instead of letting those restrictions decide what we could enjoy, we started finding ways to make food exciting, delicious, and completely our own.

And that's how our love affair with charcuterie began.

OUR STORY

How It All Began

We fell in love with the endless possibilities. The flavors, the textures, the unexpected pairings, and the freedom to create something a little different every time. A board could be a cozy night in, the star of a dinner party, the perfect companion to a good bottle of wine, or simply an excuse to gather around the table a little longer.

We realized that charcuterie wasn't just about putting delicious things on a board. It was about what those things made possible.

What began as a personal passion eventually grew into Beyond Charcuterie — a project built from the heart, for the people we love to gather with.

So, Why Beyond?

Because we believe there's so much more to charcuterie than charcuterie itself.

It's beyond the cheese. Beyond the meats. Beyond the beautiful arrangement and the picture-perfect presentation.

It's the wine that turns into hours of conversation. The friends who stay a little longer because there's still something delicious on the table. The date night that becomes a tradition. The celebrations, the laughter, the stories, and those little moments you wish you could bottle up and keep forever.

“The board is just the beginning. The memories are what make it special.”

WHAT WE DO

Made With Love, Meant to Be Shared

Beyond Charcuterie is a passion project built from the heart. Our goal is to create beautiful, thoughtfully curated food experiences that help people connect, celebrate, and enjoy time together.

We aren't just creating boards to look beautiful in photographs. We're creating something delicious that becomes part of someone's celebration, date night, dinner party, or cherished memory.

Whether you're planning an intimate gathering, a romantic date night, a dinner party, a milestone celebration, a corporate event, or a wine-tasting experience — we'd love to be part of your table.

Because life's too short for boring food, empty tables, and saving the good wine for a special occasion.

So, let's make something delicious, pour that glass, and give people one more reason to stay around the table.

With love (and probably a little cheese),

Mily & Dan
